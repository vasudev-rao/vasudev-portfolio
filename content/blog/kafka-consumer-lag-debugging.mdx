# Debugging Kafka Consumer Lag in Production

## 🚀 Context

In a real-time streaming pipeline (Kafka → Spark), we suddenly observed:

- Increasing Kafka consumer lag  
- Delayed data in dashboards  
- SLA violations  

This post walks through how I diagnosed and fixed the issue.

---

## ⚠️ Problem

The system initially worked fine, but under load:

- Lag kept increasing over time  
- Consumers couldn't keep up with producers  
- Data freshness dropped from seconds → minutes  

👉 Business impact:
- Delayed analytics  
- Loss of real-time visibility  

---

## 🔍 Step 1: Identify the bottleneck

First, I checked:

- Kafka consumer lag metrics  
- Spark streaming batch duration  
- Processing vs ingestion rate  

👉 Observation:
- Processing time > ingestion rate  

➡️ Clear sign: **consumer side bottleneck**

---

## 🔎 Step 2: Analyze Spark job

Using Spark UI:

- Long-running stages  
- Skewed tasks  
- High shuffle time  

👉 Root issue:
- Data skew in certain partitions  

---

## ⚠️ Step 3: Check Kafka partitioning

- Few partitions had heavy traffic  
- Others were underutilized  

👉 Result:
- Uneven load distribution  

---

### 1. Increased Kafka partitions
